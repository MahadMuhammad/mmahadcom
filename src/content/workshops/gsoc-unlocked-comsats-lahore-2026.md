  - heading: My Talk
    paragraphs:
      - "In this session, I share practical lessons from my open-source journey: finding a project that matches your interests, making your first contribution, communicating with maintainers, and preparing a clear, realistic GSoC proposal. I draw on my experience contributing to GCC Rust through GSoC in 2023 and 2024."
    items:
      - Understanding how Google Summer of Code works and how to prepare for an application cycle
      - Choosing organizations and projects that match your interests and skills
      - Building locally and making a first contribution through code, tests, or documentation
      - Asking useful questions and responding to feedback
      - Navigating large codebases and documenting your progress
      - Writing proposals with clear deliverables, milestones, and evidence of your work
